Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a landing page and a website?

A landing page is a single page with one goal. A website has several pages that cover your business more broadly.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. A landing page can grow into a full business website when you are ready.

How do I get started?

Send your requirements through the project form. The Starter package is a one-page website.
