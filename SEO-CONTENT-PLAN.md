# 30-day content plan (quality over volume)

Publish 6 original articles, one about every 4 to 5 days. Each needs real detail from your own work (steps you actually follow, ranges you actually charge, screenshots of real projects). Do not publish thin or templated posts, and do not repeat keywords unnaturally. Add each article to `sitemap.xml` and link it from the matching service page.

| Day | Article | Searcher intent | Link to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | Set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster, submit sitemap | (task, not an article) | - |
| 5 | How to create a professional business website: a step-by-step guide | Beginners planning a site | Business Website Development |
| 10 | Website development cost in India: what affects the price | Cost research | Pricing, cost calculator |
| 15 | Business website vs e-commerce website: which do you need? | Comparison | Business and E-commerce pages |
| 20 | Features every business website needs | Checklist | Business Website Development |
| 25 | How to choose a website development agency | Buyer evaluation | Contact / project form |
| 30 | How to improve website speed and basic SEO | Owners of existing sites | Website Maintenance |

## Rules for each article
- One clear question answered in the first paragraph, then headings that match follow-up questions.
- Use only prices and timelines you can stand behind; say they vary by scope.
- Add a short author line (Tejpratap) and the date. Update dates only when content really changes.
- End with one relevant call to action (project form), not a keyword list.
- Add Article structured data only if the page shows the same author and dates.

## After 30 days
Check Search Console: which queries show impressions, which pages are indexed. Write the next articles from real queries, not guesses.
